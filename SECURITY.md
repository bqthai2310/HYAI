# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public issues, pull requests, fixtures, or evidence. Report them privately to the Product Owner or delegated security authority with reproduction details, affected versions, impact, and any available mitigation.

## Security baseline

HYAI follows least privilege, short-lived credentials, workload isolation, attributable actions, auditability, supply-chain integrity, and deny-by-default treatment of sensitive actions. Secrets must come from an approved secret broker and must not be committed to this repository.

Security-sensitive changes require applicable acceptance evidence and independent review before promotion.
